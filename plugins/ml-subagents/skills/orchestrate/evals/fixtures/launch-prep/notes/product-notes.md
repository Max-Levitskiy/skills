# Product notes — v2.0 (source of truth for launch claims)

Confirmed for v2.0 (ship on launch day, 2026-08-04):
- Vault sync (end-to-end encrypted), all platforms: macOS, Windows, iOS, Android, web.
- Smart capture: webpages and PDFs. (Email capture slipped to v2.1 — do NOT claim it.)
- Workspaces for up to 10 people.
- Import from Evernote and Notion (one-click).
- Offline mode on mobile.

EAP results (May–July, ~400 users):
- 92% weekly retention among EAP users.
- Median sync time across devices: under 2 seconds.
- Top-requested missing feature: email capture (coming v2.1, no public date).

Known limitations (be honest if asked, don't volunteer in the announcement):
- Workspaces cap at 10 members; larger teams on a waitlist.
- Smart capture struggles with scanned/image-only PDFs.

Support: help.nimbusnotes.app. Press contact: press@nimbusnotes.app.
