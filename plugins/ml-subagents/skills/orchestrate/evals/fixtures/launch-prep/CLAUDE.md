# Nimbus Notes — launch prep workspace

Small internal workspace for preparing the Nimbus Notes v2.0 public launch.

## Conventions

- **Read `glossary.md` before any doc work.** It is the canonical vocabulary; some
  acronym expansions are marked unconfirmed — never present those as confirmed.
- **Every claim traces.** Facts about the product or pricing come from `notes/` or are
  marked "TBD". No invented features, numbers, or dates.
- **Tone**: plain language, no marketing superlatives ("revolutionary", "game-changing").
- **Task tracking**: `tasks.md` is the single tracker. Keep statuses current there.
- Deliverables go in `deliverables/`.
